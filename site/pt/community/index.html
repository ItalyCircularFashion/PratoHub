Publication rule

PUBLISHED is the only status exposed publicly.

DRAFT, UNDER_REVIEW, VERIFIED, REJECTED and ARCHIVED are not public content. The separation also applies to linked replies and comments.

Profile
A contribution requires an approved profile with an eligible role.
Hierarchy
A reply or comment never precedes publication of the content that contains it.
Reactions
They are allowed only on published posts and are not exposed as launch counters.

Publication rule

Before contributing

The forum prioritises contextual questions, declared experience and professional language. Moderation may request clarification, defer or reject a text.

  1. State the technical or professional topic and context precisely.
  2. Avoid unnecessary personal data, confidential references and unverifiable statements.
  3. Wait for the review result before treating a contribution as public.
Why is there no discussion form on this page?

Submission is activated only when identity, privacy, a moderation queue and real persistence are operational. No text is retained locally as a substitute for the service.
