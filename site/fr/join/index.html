Service status

Conditions to complete before opening

  1. Register the API with a real persistence adapter: no mock or in-memory saving.
  2. Bind every authenticated request to its actual profile instead of accepting author or moderator identifiers from the browser.
  3. Define a privacy notice, retention periods, abuse controls and a data-subject request channel.
  4. Provide a review queue for applications and contributions before publication.

Even after approval, text stays pre-moderated.

Topics, posts and comments enter review. Only an explicit transition to PUBLISHED enables public reading; there is no auto-publication shortcut.

Why is there no disabled form?

A form that appears to submit data without operational persistence and privacy foundations creates a false expectation. The interface therefore states that the service is unavailable instead of simulating an application.
